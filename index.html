Game rule 1 of 3

Reach 50 points

The round continues until you reach 50 points. Earn points by getting other players to return the ball directly to you after you throw it to them.

+1
Every time the player you chose throws the ball straight back to you, you gain one point.

A regular throw to you does not add a point.

Game rule 2 of 3

Do not repeat your choice

You can choose any of the three players whenever you have the ball.

−2
If you choose the same player on two consecutive choices, you lose two points.

You may still choose the same player — the penalty will simply be applied.

Game rule 3 of 3

Special team throw

Sometimes the background will turn light green and the ball will become a star.

SPECIAL THROW
+2
If you pass the star back to the person who gave it to you, every player receives +2 points.
−5
If you pass the star to someone else, you lose 5 points.

This special rule overrides Rule 1 and Rule 2.

On a green/star throw, neither the ordinary +1 return rule nor the repeated-choice −2 rule applies.
